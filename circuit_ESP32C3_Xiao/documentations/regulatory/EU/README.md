# EU (CE marking) Regulatory Docs

This folder is for EU requirements and the artifacts typically expected for CE marking.

## Typical directives for electronics
Depending on the final product configuration:
- **RED (2014/53/EU)** — for products with intentional radio (Wi‑Fi/BLE/etc). Covers safety objectives, EMC, and spectrum use.
- **EMC (2014/30/EU)** — EMC requirements for apparatus (when RED does not apply).
- **LVD (2014/35/EU)** — electrical safety for equipment within certain voltage limits (mains-type voltages).
- **RoHS (2011/65/EU)** — hazardous substance restrictions for EEE.
