# Asia Regulatory Overview (starter)

## Purpose
A place to collect and normalize requirements for Asian markets as the project expands.

## Recommended structure
When a specific market is in-scope, create a country folder and add:
- A one-page overview of the approval scheme
- Required artifacts checklist (test reports, label text, manuals)
- Links to official sources

## Markets (to be expanded)
- Japan
- China
- Korea
- Taiwan
- Singapore

## Notes
Avoid copying standards text into the repo. Prefer storing your own checklists and linking to the authoritative sources.

## References
- Japan MIC (radio regulations / certification info): https://www.tele.soumu.go.jp/e/index.htm
- China MIIT (regulatory entry point): https://www.miit.gov.cn/
- Korea RRA (certification authority): https://www.rra.go.kr/
- Taiwan NCC: https://www.ncc.gov.tw/
